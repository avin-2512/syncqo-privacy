Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

The short version: StorkLabs runs no servers and has no backend. Our apps — including Syncqo — do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data with us. Your files and credentials stay on your device and move only between you and the third-party services you choose to connect. Syncqo uses Google Analytics (Firebase) to see anonymized, aggregate feature usage, and its Free tier offers an optional rewarded ad served by Google AdMob — see Advertising and Analytics below for both.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how StorkLabs ("StorkLabs", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile applications we publish, including Syncqo (each, an "App", and together the "Apps"). It applies to the Apps and this website.

The Apps are designed to be backend-less: StorkLabs does not operate servers that receive, process, or store your content or personal information. Because of this architecture, the vast majority of "data handling" described in typical privacy policies simply does not occur here.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. StorkLabs does not have accounts of our own, and does not receive your files, folder names, cloud credentials, contacts, or location. Syncqo does use Google Analytics (Firebase) for anonymized, aggregate product usage, and its Free tier shows an optional rewarded ad — both described in Advertising and Analytics below.

  • No account. You do not create a StorkLabs account and do not give us an email, name, or password.
  • No behavioral ad tracking. We do not embed third-party trackers for cross-app or cross-site behavioral profiling.
  • No content access. The files you transfer, and the credentials you use to reach your clouds, are never sent to StorkLabs.

Limited, non-identifying diagnostic information (such as crash reports or aggregate install and purchase counts) may be made available to us by the app store or operating system. That information is collected and controlled by those platforms under their own policies, not by StorkLabs, and we use it only to keep the Apps working.

3. Data stored on your device

To function, the Apps store data locally on your device only. This may include the cloud connections you set up, access tokens issued by your cloud providers, your transfer history and settings, and cached files created during a transfer. In Syncqo, sensitive configuration such as cloud credentials is protected using the operating system's secure key storage where available.

StorkLabs cannot see, retrieve, or recover this on-device data. It remains under your control and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

4. Third-party services

The Apps let you connect to and interact with third-party services that you choose. When you use those services, your information is handled by them under their privacy policies and terms, over which StorkLabs has no control and for which StorkLabs is not responsible. These may include:

  • Cloud storage providers (for example Google Drive, Google Photos, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, and similar), which you authorize directly via secure sign-in (OAuth). Your files and tokens flow between your device and those providers, not through StorkLabs.
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Services, which process purchases, subscriptions, and (if used) sign-in. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; StorkLabs never receives your card or payment information.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any service you connect. Your use of those services is at your own discretion and risk.

5. Advertising

Syncqo's Free tier offers an optional way to skip a wait by watching a short rewarded video ad ("Watch ad to unblock"). This is entirely opt-in — nothing plays unless you choose to watch one, and Pro subscribers never see ads at all. Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID and other technical identifiers to select and deliver ads and to prevent fraud, per Google's own advertising and privacy policies. For users in the EEA/UK, ads are only requested after you respond to a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) governing that data use. This data is handled by Google's infrastructure, not by StorkLabs — we never receive or see it ourselves. You can review or reset your advertising ID, and control ad personalization generally, in your device's Google Settings → Ads.

6. Analytics

Syncqo uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in the aggregate, how the app is used — which screens are opened, which features (like connecting a cloud, creating a scheduled transfer, or completing a transfer) are used, and how often people sign in or subscribe. This is pseudonymous, product-improvement data, not an identity profile: it is tied to an anonymized, one-way hashed identifier derived from your account (never your raw email address or name), not to you personally, and it never includes your files, folder names, or cloud credentials. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy and data-processing terms. It is separate from the AdMob advertising data described above, even though both are Google products.

7. Nearby sharing (InstaSend / InstaReceive)

Syncqo's nearby-sharing features transfer files directly between devices over your local Wi-Fi network. These transfers do not pass through StorkLabs and are not stored by us. You are responsible for the network you use and for who you share with. Anyone with the sharing link or QR code on the same network during an active session may be able to download the shared content.

8. Device permissions

The Apps may request device permissions (such as access to files/storage, notifications, camera for scanning QR codes, or network access) solely to provide the features you invoke. These permissions are used on-device to perform the requested action; the data accessed is not transmitted to StorkLabs. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

9. Children

The Apps are not directed to children and are not intended for use by anyone under the age required to form a binding contract or the minimum age of digital consent in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Security

We design the Apps to keep sensitive data on your device and to use platform security features where available. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The Apps are provided "as is", and to the maximum extent permitted by law StorkLabs disclaims responsibility for unauthorized access to, or loss, misuse, or corruption of, data on your device, on your networks, or on third-party services. You are responsible for securing your device, your networks, and your accounts.

11. Data retention & deletion

Because we do not collect your personal data, there is nothing for StorkLabs to retain or delete on your behalf. In Syncqo, Settings → Account → "Delete my data" revokes the app's Google sign-in access and permanently erases the encrypted cloud credentials stored on your device — no uninstall required. Uninstalling the App also removes everything. To disconnect a single cloud provider instead, revoke the App's access from that provider's own account/security settings.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Apps after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
