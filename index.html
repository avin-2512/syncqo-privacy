Syncqo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Syncqo ("the app", "we") is a personal cloud transfer tool that runs entirely on your device.

This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is used, and what leaves your device.

The short version

  • Syncqo has no backend server of our own. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or

process your files or personal data.

  • Your files move directly between your device and the cloud storage accounts you connect

(e.g. Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, S3). We are not a middleman for that data.

  • Credentials for your cloud accounts are stored encrypted on your device and never transmitted

to us.

Information the app accesses

Google account (sign-in). Syncqo uses Google Sign-In to let you into the app. We receive your

basic Google profile (email address and name) from Google for the purpose of authenticating you and

enabling your subscription entitlement. This information is used on your device and is **not sent to

any server operated by us**.

Cloud storage credentials. When you connect a cloud provider, the access tokens or keys for that

provider are stored encrypted at rest on your device (hardware-backed keystore) and are used

only to perform the transfers you request. They are never sent to us.

Files and folders you choose to transfer. To move, back up, or sync your data, the app reads the

files and folders you select (on your phone and/or in your connected clouds) and writes them to the

destination you choose. This content flows directly between your device and your cloud accounts. We

do not receive, retain, or inspect it.

Local network (InstaSend). For nearby device-to-device transfers, the app runs a temporary local

web server on your Wi-Fi network so another device can receive a file you send. App-to-app transfers

are encrypted; transfers to a plain browser are unencrypted on the local network by design.

Advertising

Syncqo's Free tier offers an optional way to skip a wait by watching a short rewarded video ad ("Watch ad to unblock"). This is entirely opt-in — nothing plays unless you choose to watch one, and Pro subscribers never see ads at all. Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID and other technical identifiers to select and deliver ads and to prevent fraud, per Google's own advertising and privacy policies. For users in the EEA/UK, ads are only requested after you respond to a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) governing that data use. This ad data is handled by Google's infrastructure, not ours — see "Information we collect on our servers" below for what that distinction means in practice. You can review or reset your advertising ID, and control ad personalization generally, in your device's Google Settings → Ads.

Information we collect on our servers

None. Syncqo (the developer) does not operate our own analytics or data-collection backend, and we do not build user profiles. The one exception is the AdMob SDK described above: when a Free-tier user opts to watch a rewarded ad, Google's ad infrastructure (not ours) collects the advertising data needed to serve that ad. We never see or receive that data ourselves.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment

details. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions we request and why

  • All files access / media access — to let you browse and back up files and folders on your

device that you select.

  • Camera — only to scan an InstaSend QR code when receiving a file.
  • Notifications — to show transfer progress and completion.
  • Network / foreground service — to run and complete transfers reliably, including in the

background.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

To remove all app data from your device, sign out and uninstall the app; this deletes the encrypted

credential store and all local app data. To revoke Syncqo's access to a cloud provider, remove its

authorization in that provider's account settings.

Children

Syncqo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
