Delete your Syncqo data

Last updated: September 28, 2026

The short version: Syncqo has no StorkLabs account and no server holding your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. You can erase everything Syncqo keeps for you, on your own phone, in about 10 seconds, without uninstalling.

How to delete your data in the app

  1. Open Syncqo on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the profile icon (top-right of the Home screen) and choose Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete my data.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete everything.

Syncqo then revokes its Google sign-in access, permanently erases the encrypted cloud credentials stored on your device, and restarts signed out. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted, and what is not

Deleted immediately

  • Every cloud connection you added (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, and the rest) and the access tokens and keys behind them, in both their encrypted and working copies.
  • Syncqo's Google sign-in access to your Google account (revoked).

Not affected

  • Your files in your clouds. Syncqo never stored them, so they are untouched.
  • Local app data on your phone such as transfer history, saved schedules, and preferences. Remove these by uninstalling Syncqo, or in Android Settings → Apps → Syncqo → Storage → Clear storage.

Prefer to uninstall?

Uninstalling Syncqo removes all of its data from your device. To also cut off Syncqo's access to a single cloud provider, revoke Syncqo from that provider's own account or security settings.

Retention and analytics

StorkLabs holds no copy of your files, credentials, email address, or name, so there is no retention period on our side. Syncqo does send anonymized usage events (screens opened, features used) to Google Analytics for Firebase, keyed to a one-way hash of your account rather than your email or name; see the Analytics section of our Privacy Policy. Google keeps this data for the period configured in our analytics settings. To ask us to have your usage data removed, email avin.2512@gmail.com from any address and say so.
